Frequently Asked Questions

How are players selected?

Eligible and verified registrations may be evaluated and placed into the draft pool. Franchise owners select players through the live auction process.

Does registration guarantee selection?

No. Registration allows a player to be evaluated and considered, but draft selection is not guaranteed.

What happens if a player is unsold?

TNYPL may reopen unsold players in a later round or manage them according to the published draft rules.

How do owners join the auction?

Approved founding owners activate their secure portal, create a password and use the owner auction console.

Where can the public watch?

The public draft room displays live auction data and, when configured, the official livestream.
