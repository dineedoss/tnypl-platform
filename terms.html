TERMS & CONDITIONS

Player Registration Terms

Registration is subject to eligibility, document verification, payment verification and acceptance by TNYPL administration. Registration does not guarantee selection by a franchise.

Eligibility

Players must be born between 01 January 2010 and 31 December 2012, both dates inclusive, and must provide valid age proof.

Accuracy and conduct

Incorrect, misleading or fraudulent information may result in rejection or removal from the draft. Players and guardians agree to follow tournament rules, safeguarding expectations and standards of conduct.

Media

Separate consent should be obtained for identifiable player photography, interviews and promotional use, especially because participants are minors.

This operational notice should be reviewed against applicable legal requirements before public launch.
