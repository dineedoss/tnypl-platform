# TNYPL 2027 Brand Guide

## Positioning
**TNYPL 2027 — The New Era**

Season Two should look distinct from the inaugural season while retaining the TNYPL name.

## Logo system
1. Primary circular crest — digital hero, jerseys, trophies, social graphics
2. Horizontal wordmark — website nav, email, sponsor decks
3. Monochrome crest — embroidery, black/white print, watermark
4. Favicon — browser and app icon

## Typography
- Headlines: Bebas Neue
- Editorial / brand copy: Space Grotesk
- Interface/body: Inter

## Palette
- Midnight: `#07111F`
- Deep Card: `#102746`
- Electric Cyan: `#00D4FF`
- Ultraviolet: `#7B61FF`
- Championship Gold: `#FFD166`
- Ice White: `#F8FAFC`
- Slate: `#8DA7C4`

## Motion language
- 400–900ms
- cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
- subtle 3D logo response on desktop
- orbital brand motion
- controlled scroll reveal
- no bounce or flashing effects
