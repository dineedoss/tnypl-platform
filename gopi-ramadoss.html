FOUNDING OWNER · Kovai Kings

Gopi Ramadoss

Gopi Ramadoss is the Founding Owner of Kovai Kings.

Owner Profile

As Founding Owner of Kovai Kings, Gopi supports the development of a professionally managed youth-cricket franchise built around opportunity, team identity, competitive spirit and responsible leadership.

Franchise Vision

Kovai Kings aims to provide young cricketers with a platform where performance, discipline and teamwork are valued. The franchise's inaugural-season focus is to build a balanced squad, create a positive team environment and give players the confidence to compete at a higher standard.

Owner Responsibilities

  • Franchise leadership and team identity
  • Supporting the player-selection and draft process
  • Encouraging player welfare and youth development
  • Working collaboratively with TNYPL officials
  • Promoting fair play, discipline and sportsmanship
  • Building a strong foundation for future seasons

Leadership Approach

The Kovai Kings ownership approach is centered on giving young players a structured environment in which they can learn, compete and grow. Additional professional biography can be incorporated when formally supplied by the owner.
