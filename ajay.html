TNYPL LEADERSHIP

Ajay Manohar

Senior Coach & Tournament Administration and Player Services Coordinator

With nearly 15 years of association with Kingstar Academy, Ajay began his coaching journey as a Junior Assistant Coach, progressed to Assistant Coach and now serves as Senior Coach.

Cricket & Coaching Journey

Ajay currently coaches Under-16, Under-19 and Open Category players, specialising in one-on-one batting coaching. A right-hand opening batsman and wicketkeeper, he has represented TNCA Fifth Division and TDCA First Division cricket and currently represents a TNCA Sixth Division team.

As an active cricketer and passionate coach, he continues to play the game while mentoring and developing aspiring players.

TNYPL Responsibilities

  • Player registration and parent communication
  • Documentation and accreditation support
  • Match-day player services and check-in
  • Volunteer and operational coordination
  • Supporting Ranjith Janagiraman and the Tournament Operations team
  • Coaching and talent-development support

VISION

“Every young cricketer deserves a professional environment to learn, compete and grow. My goal is to help players develop the skills, discipline, confidence and character needed to succeed on and off the field.”
