Tournament Format

How the League
Works

📋

Round Robin

All six teams play each other once in a round-robin group stage across the first four days — 15 matches total.

⚡

T20 Format

Every match is a 20-over T20 fixture. Fast, exciting, and packed with skill — exactly the format young players need.

📅

Five Days

Three matches per day across four group-stage days, followed by a finals day featuring the Grand Final and Third-Place Play-off.

👶

U13 – U14 Only

Strict date-of-birth verification ensures only eligible U13 and U14 players compete. Fair, transparent, and rigorous.

👥

13-Player Squads

Each franchise drafts exactly 13 players — 78 young cricketers in total. Every squad spot matters.

🎟

Free Entry

Spectators attend all matches free of charge. Families, selectors, and cricket fans are all welcome at every game.
