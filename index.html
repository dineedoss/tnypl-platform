PRIVACY & VERIFICATION NOTICE How we handle age-proof documents

TNYPL collects player and parent/guardian information only for registration, age eligibility, payment verification, tournament administration and the player draft. Aadhaar is not mandatory if another accepted age-proof document is available. Please mask the first eight Aadhaar digits wherever practical and upload only the minimum document needed for verification.

Documents are stored in private access-controlled storage, reviewed only by authorized TNYPL administrators, and are not displayed publicly. Verification documents will be securely deleted after the published retention period, subject to legal, dispute-resolution or financial-record requirements.
