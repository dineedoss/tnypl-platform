OFFICIAL PLAYER DRAFT · 25 AUGUST 2026

The TNYPL
Draft Process

Six franchises. Equal points. Verified players. Transparent selection.

1. Registration and verification

Players submit their application, profile photo, parent or guardian information, age proof, payment receipt and CricHeroes profile. TNYPL administration validates eligibility, documents and payment before confirming draft-pool status.

2. Technical evaluation

The Technical Committee reviews verified players using experience, CricHeroes performance, role, recent form and technical assessment. Players are assigned Platinum, Gold or Silver category.

3. Equal franchise points

Every franchise begins with the same points budget. Category minimums, squad-size requirements and role-balance rules apply so every team can build a competitive squad.

4. Live points-based draft

On 25 August 2026, player photos and approved profiles appear in the live Draft Centre. Owners bid or select using their remaining points. Each confirmed pick updates the franchise squad and budget immediately.

5. Draft outcome

Selected players receive a welcome message naming their franchise. Verified players not selected receive an encouraging communication and refund information according to the published policy.

Recommended inaugural rules

1,000 points per franchise · 13-player squads · maximum 3 Platinum players · minimum 4 Silver players · at least 1 wicketkeeper · at least 4 bowling options · at least 2 all-rounders.
