ABOUT THE OWNERS

Two leaders. One shared purpose.

Saravanan Narasimhan and Santhana Krishnan are the proud Founding Joint Owners of Thanjavur Royals, united by a shared vision of nurturing young cricketing talent and building a professionally managed franchise within TNYPL.

Bringing together business leadership and corporate-finance experience, they are committed to creating an environment where young athletes develop their skills, compete with confidence and grow on and off the field.

Saravanan Narasimhan brings a passion for leadership, community engagement and youth development. Santhana Krishnan, a Finance Manager at Atos with more than twenty years of corporate experience, contributes expertise in financial management, governance and strategic planning.

JOINT VISION

As Founding Joint Owners of Thanjavur Royals, our vision is to build more than a successful cricket franchise. We aspire to create a platform where young cricketers dream bigger, develop their potential and represent their communities with pride.
