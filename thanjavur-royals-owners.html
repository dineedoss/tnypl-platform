FOUNDING OWNER · Thanjavur Royals

Saravanan Narasimhan & Santhana Krishnan

Saravanan Narasimhan and Santhana Krishnan are the proud Founding Co-Owners of Thanjavur Royals, united by a shared vision of nurturing young cricketing talent and building a professionally managed TNYPL franchise.

About Saravanan Narasimhan

Saravanan is a business professional with a passion for leadership, community engagement and youth development.

About Santhana Krishnan

Santhana Krishnan is a Finance Manager at Atos with more than 20 years of corporate experience, contributing expertise in financial management, governance and strategic planning.

Shared Values

Together, they believe success is built through discipline, teamwork, integrity and creating meaningful opportunities for the next generation of cricketers.

Joint Vision

Their vision is to build more than a successful franchise: a platform where young cricketers can dream bigger, develop their potential and represent their communities with pride. Through professional management and a commitment to excellence, they aim to make Thanjavur Royals a benchmark franchise in TNYPL.
