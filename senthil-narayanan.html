Technical Director

Senthil Narayanan

Kingstar Academy · Chennai

Senthil Narayanan serves as TNYPL Technical Director, leading cricket operations, player-evaluation standards, technical decision-making and the official live player draft. He works with the league's coaches and administrators to maintain a consistent, transparent and cricket-focused assessment process for every registered player.

Role & Focus

  • Player evaluation and grading standards
  • Technical oversight of the selection process
  • CricHeroes profile and performance review
  • Platinum, Gold and Silver tier recommendations
  • Draft eligibility and player classification
  • Coordination with coaches and selectors
  • Player-role assessment and team-balance guidance
  • Technical decisions during the official live player draft
  • Cricket operations and tournament standards
  • Fairness and consistency across player evaluations

Technical Responsibilities

Senthil provides technical guidance throughout the TNYPL player pathway, from registration review and performance assessment through classification, draft preparation and final selection. His role is to help ensure that every franchise receives access to a professionally evaluated and clearly structured player pool.

Selection Philosophy

TNYPL player assessment considers available performance data together with cricketing judgement. Statistics are important, but technique, consistency, match awareness, role suitability, temperament, fielding and development potential also matter when forming a complete view of a young player.

Areas of Expertise

  • Youth player evaluation
  • Talent identification
  • Performance analysis
  • Technical cricket standards
  • Draft planning and player classification
  • Cricket operations
