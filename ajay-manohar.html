Coaching Background

Ajay has nearly 15 years of association with Kingstar Academy, progressing from Junior Assistant Coach to Assistant Coach and Senior Coach. He currently works with Under-16, Under-19 and Open-category players and has a special focus on individual batting development.

Role & Focus

  • Tournament administration and cricket coordination
  • Youth batting development and one-on-one coaching
  • Player preparation and match-day readiness
  • Technical compliance and playing standards
  • Communication across coaches, players and administrators
  • Right-hand opening batting experience
  • Wicketkeeping experience
  • TNCA Fifth Division and TDCA First Division experience

Contribution to TNYPL

Ajay supports the connection between technical coaching and tournament administration. His role helps ensure that player development, match preparation and operational requirements remain aligned as TNYPL moves from registration and selection into competition.

Coaching & Administration Philosophy

Strong tournaments require both quality cricket and disciplined organization. Ajay's approach emphasizes preparation, clear communication, consistent standards and creating an environment in which young players can focus on performing to the best of their ability.
