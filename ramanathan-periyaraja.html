FOUNDING OWNER · Nellai Falcons

Ramanathan Periyaraja

Ramanathan Periyaraja is the Founding Owner of Nellai Falcons.

Franchise Vision

Through Nellai Falcons, Ramanathan supports a competitive platform that gives young players the opportunity to perform, develop and gain visibility. The franchise aims to build a strong identity around preparation, teamwork, resilience and pride in representing the Nellai cricket community.

Owner Responsibilities

  • Franchise leadership and long-term direction
  • Supporting player opportunity and development
  • Building team culture and franchise identity
  • Working with TNYPL officials through the draft and tournament
  • Encouraging fair play, discipline and sportsmanship
  • Creating a positive environment for young athletes

Leadership Approach

The Nellai Falcons ownership approach is focused on providing players with a professionally organized environment in which effort, performance and character are recognized. Additional professional and community biography can be incorporated when formally supplied.
