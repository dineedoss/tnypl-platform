Coaching Background

Ranjith has been associated with Kingstar Academy for nearly a decade, progressing from Assistant Coach to Senior Coach. He works across Under-14, Under-16, Under-19 and Open categories and places particular emphasis on individual bowling development.

Role & Focus

  • Youth player development and individual improvement
  • One-on-one bowling coaching and skill refinement
  • Technical assessment of young bowlers
  • Pre-draft player observation and development feedback
  • Match preparation, discipline and cricket awareness
  • Right-handed middle-order batting experience
  • Right-arm spin bowling experience
  • TNCA Sixth Division and CDCA Second Division experience

Contribution to TNYPL

Within TNYPL, Ranjith supports player-development standards and helps the technical team view young cricketers through a coaching lens. His contribution emphasizes fundamentals, repeatable skills, match readiness and the long-term development of each player rather than relying on statistics alone.

Coaching Philosophy

Development is a process built through disciplined practice, clear feedback and the confidence to apply skills under match pressure. Ranjith's focus is to help young players understand their game, improve their strengths and work constructively on areas that need attention.
