Draft & Auction Rules

Player Categories

Approved players may be classified as Platinum, Gold or Silver based on evaluation and league criteria.

Opening Points and Increments

Each lot has published opening points. Minimum increments are enforced by the auction system and may vary according to the current bid.

Timer and Extensions

The Commissioner controls the timer. Late valid bids may extend the bidding window to allow a fair response.

Sold and Unsold

A player is sold only after the Commissioner confirms the result. A player may be marked unsold when no valid bid is received.

Franchise Purse and Squad Limit

Every franchise must remain within its permitted bidding capacity and squad limit. The platform may block invalid bids automatically.

Technical Decisions

During connection issues or disputes, the Commissioner may pause, reset, reopen or manually correct an auction lot to preserve fairness.
