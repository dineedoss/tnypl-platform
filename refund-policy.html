REFUND POLICY

Registration Fee Refund

A verified player who enters the official TNYPL draft pool but is not selected by any franchise is eligible for a refund of the ₹2,000 registration fee, subject to the published process.

Not normally eligible

Applications rejected for ineligibility, failed document or payment checks, inaccurate information, duplicate registration or voluntary withdrawal may not qualify.

Processing

Refund eligibility will be confirmed after the player draft. Approved refunds will be recorded by TNYPL administration and processed to the verified payment source or another approved method.
