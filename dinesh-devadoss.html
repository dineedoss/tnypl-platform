Founder & Chairman

Dinesh Devadoss

Dallas, Texas, USA

Dinesh Devadoss is the Founder & Chairman of TNYPL and Owner of the Dallas Thunders franchise in the US Junior League. He is focused on building a transparent, professionally managed youth-cricket platform where opportunity is earned through performance.

Meet the Founder

Inspired by his experience in franchise cricket and youth development in the United States, Dinesh founded TNYPL to create a professional, transparent and technology-driven platform for aspiring cricketers across Tamil Nadu.

Chairman's Vision

“Every young cricketer deserves a fair opportunity to showcase their talent. TNYPL is committed to creating that platform through professionalism, transparency and excellence.”

Our Mission

  • Develop the next generation of Tamil Nadu cricketers.
  • Conduct fair and transparent player selections.
  • Provide professional tournament standards.
  • Deliver live scoring, statistics and digital player profiles.
  • Connect players with coaches, academies and future opportunities.

Chairman's Message

“TNYPL was created with a simple belief—that talent should never be limited by opportunity. Every player deserves a professional stage where hard work, discipline and performance determine success. This is only the beginning.”
