PRIVACY POLICY

Player and Parent Data

TNYPL collects information required for player registration, age and payment verification, communication, tournament administration and the official player draft.

Age proof and Aadhaar

Aadhaar is not mandatory when another accepted age-proof document is available. Applicants should mask the first eight Aadhaar digits wherever practical and upload only the minimum information necessary for verification.

Storage and access

Documents are stored in private access-controlled storage. Only authorized TNYPL administrators may access them for verification. Documents are not published or shared with franchise owners; owners receive only approved player-profile information required for the draft.

Retention and deletion

Verification documents will be securely deleted after the published retention period following the tournament, unless retention is reasonably required for legal obligations, financial reconciliation, safeguarding, complaints or disputes.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests may be sent to info@tnypl.in.
