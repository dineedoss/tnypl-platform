FOUNDING OWNER · Tiruppur Blazers

P. C. Binny Jo

P. C. Binny Jo is the Founding Owner of Tiruppur Blazers and a Partner at Shalom Enterprises.

Professional Background

He is associated with an IOCL petrol pump, Servo stockist operations and CEAT distribution in Chennai.

Franchise Role

As Founding Owner of Tiruppur Blazers, Binny supports the development of a high-energy franchise identity and a professionally managed youth-cricket opportunity. His business background contributes an operational and relationship-focused perspective to franchise leadership.

Owner Vision

The Tiruppur Blazers vision is to build a competitive, disciplined and energetic squad while ensuring young players receive a positive tournament experience. The franchise aims to encourage confidence, teamwork, responsibility and the ability to perform under pressure.

Owner Responsibilities

  • Franchise leadership and strategic direction
  • Supporting player selection and squad development
  • Building a positive team culture
  • Working with TNYPL league and technical officials
  • Supporting player welfare and professional standards
  • Developing the Tiruppur Blazers identity for future seasons
