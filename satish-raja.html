Satish Raja

Founding Owner · Trichy Titans

TRICHY TITANS

Building Future Champions

Satish Raja is an educator, mentor and passionate cricket supporter who believes sport can develop leadership, discipline and character in young people.

As the Founding Owner of Trichy Titans, he is committed to building a professional environment where talented young cricketers can compete, gain exposure and realize their potential.

Franchise Values

Opportunity. Education. Discipline. Teamwork. Sportsmanship. Long-term player development.

Owner Responsibilities

As Founding Owner, Satish provides franchise leadership, supports the player-selection process, encourages a strong team culture and works with TNYPL officials to create a professional environment for young players. The Trichy Titans approach places equal value on competitive ambition, discipline, player welfare and personal development.

Franchise Vision

Trichy Titans aims to develop a squad that competes with confidence while representing the values of preparation, respect and responsibility. The broader goal is to give young cricketers meaningful exposure and experiences that help them grow both on and off the field.

“Every young cricketer deserves an opportunity to showcase their talent. Trichy Titans will compete with ambition while helping players become confident, disciplined and responsible athletes.”
