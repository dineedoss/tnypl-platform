BUILDING MORE THAN A CRICKET LEAGUE

Talent should be found by merit—not limited by access.

When TNYPL was envisioned, the goal was never to create just another tournament. The purpose was to build a professional youth-cricket ecosystem where talent is identified transparently, opportunities are earned through performance, and aspiring players receive a platform that treats their ambitions seriously.

Too many capable young cricketers remain unseen because they lack exposure, guidance or access to the right network. TNYPL exists to help bridge that gap through verified registration, structured evaluation, franchise participation, documented performance and a visible player draft.

OUR MISSION

Discover talent.
Develop character.
Create opportunity.

01

Transparency

Registration, verification, evaluation and selection should follow a clear and accountable process.

02

Equal Opportunity

A player's district, academy or personal network should not determine whether their ability is seen.

03

Professional Excellence

Technology, administration, safeguarding and match presentation should reflect the seriousness of the players' aspirations.

04

Youth Development

The league should nurture discipline, confidence, teamwork, responsibility and leadership—not only cricketing skill.

05

Integrity

Every decision should protect fairness, player welfare and the long-term credibility of TNYPL.

06

Community Impact

Franchises, academies, coaches, parents and sponsors should work together to expand meaningful pathways for young athletes.

WHY TNYPL MATTERS

A visible pathway for players who might otherwise be overlooked.

✓ Verified player registration and age eligibility

✓ Professional franchise identities and accountable ownership

✓ Transparent live player draft

✓ Digital player and CricHeroes visibility

✓ Structured match records and performance recognition

✓ Partnerships with coaches, academies and schools

✓ A platform that can grow into mentoring, scholarships and wider scouting

LONG-TERM VISION

From an inaugural tournament to a sustainable cricket ecosystem.

FOUNDING SEASON2026

Launch six franchises, a verified player pool, a live draft and a professionally presented five-day tournament.

STATEWIDE REACHNEXT

Expand scouting and partnerships across more districts, academies, schools and community cricket programs.

PLAYER DEVELOPMENTGROW

Introduce structured mentoring, performance feedback, digital records and development opportunities.

FUTURE LEAGUESBUILD

Explore additional age groups, women's cricket, scholarship pathways and stronger connections to higher-level competition.

A MESSAGE TO EVERY PLAYER

Your background should never define your future. Your preparation, discipline and performance should.

Respect the game. Keep learning. Support your teammates. Accept feedback. Stay ready for the opportunity you are working toward.
